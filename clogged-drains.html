Signs you need it

  • Water drains slowlyUsually a clog building up in the trap or the branch line.
  • Gurgling soundsAir pushing past a partial blockage.
  • Bad smell from the drainGrease, food or hair breaking down inside the pipe.
  • More than one drain backs upThat points to the main line. See sewer lines.
